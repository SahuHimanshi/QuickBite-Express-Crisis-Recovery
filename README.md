# 🍔 QuickBite Express – Crisis Recovery Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

QuickBite Express experienced a major business crisis that resulted in declining revenue, customer attrition, and operational challenges.

The objective of this project was to identify the root causes of the decline and recommend high-impact recovery actions through data-driven analysis.

The analysis was built using **149K+ order records**, evaluated **20K restaurant partners**, and presented through a **5-page interactive Power BI dashboard**.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

This project focused on answering five key business questions:

- 📉 How severely did the crisis impact the business?
- 👥 Which customers should be prioritized for recovery?
- 🍽️ Which restaurant partners should be retained, improved, or replaced?
- 🚚 Were delivery operations the primary driver of the crisis?
- ⭐ What factors had the greatest impact on customer trust?

# 🔍 Investigation Summary

## 📉 Crisis Impact

<img src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/d6341e93-08dd-4e19-be38-8c6590339c6b" width="900"/>

Key findings:

- Revenue declined by nearly **71%**
- Orders dropped by approximately **69%**
- Bengaluru contributed the highest revenue loss

---

## 👥 Customer Recovery

<img src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/4247a098-2ec6-4706-a2e7-909dadafb748" width="900"/>

Key findings:

- Around **73K customers** became inactive during the crisis
- Bengaluru offered the largest recovery opportunity
- Identified a recoverable customer segment for targeted win-back campaigns

---

## 🍽️ Restaurant Strategy

<img src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/09df190e-5fd8-4500-a639-495775533861" width="900"/>

Key findings:

- Only **5.24%** of partners met all operational quality benchmarks
- Nearly **93%** required operational improvement
- Performance issues were platform-wide rather than partner-type specific

---

## 🚚 Delivery Operations

<img src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/3454bdc4-e7f1-416a-890f-0c1952b75fc0" width="900"/>

Key findings:

- SLA breaches increased significantly during the crisis
- Delivery distance, vehicle type, and employment type showed minimal performance variation
- Platform-wide operational improvements were recommended

---

## ⭐ Customer Trust

<img src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/71dacc29-4830-4b5d-a8e6-9776ff806ffd" width="900"/>

Key findings:

- Food Quality complaints increased from **8.78% → 39.37%**
- Delivery Experience complaints declined from **19.87% → 7.27%**
- Food Quality & Hygiene emerged as the primary driver of declining customer trust
