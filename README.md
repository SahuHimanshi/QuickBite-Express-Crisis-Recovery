🍔 QuickBite Express – Crisis Recovery Analytics Dashboard
Project Overview

QuickBite Express experienced a major business crisis that resulted in declining revenue, customer attrition, and operational challenges.

The objective of this project was to identify the root causes of the decline and recommend high-impact recovery actions through data-driven analysis.

The analysis was built using 149K+ order records, 20K restaurant partners, and presented through a 5-page interactive Power BI dashboard.

Business Problem

The analysis focused on answering five key business questions:

📉 How severely did the crisis impact the business?

👥 Which customers should be prioritized for recovery?

🍽️ Which restaurant partners should be retained, improved, or replaced?

🚚 Were delivery operations the primary cause of the crisis?

⭐ What factors most affected customer trust?

Key Insights

Crisis Impact
Revenue declined by nearly 71%
Orders dropped by approximately 69%
Bengaluru contributed the highest revenue loss

Customer Recovery
Around 73K customers became inactive
Bengaluru offered the largest recovery opportunity
Created a recoverable customer segment for targeted campaigns

Restaurant Strategy
Only 5.24% of partners met all quality benchmarks
Nearly 93% required operational improvements
Operational issues were platform-wide

Delivery Operations
SLA breaches increased significantly
Delivery distance, vehicle type, and employment type showed minimal variation
Platform-wide operational improvement recommended

Customer Trust
Food Quality complaints increased from 8.78% → 39.37%
Delivery complaints declined from 19.87% → 7.27%
Food Quality & Hygiene emerged as the primary driver of declining trust
